# 🗒️ Agendas

> [!tip]- How to use
> Standing agendas for people, teams, or recurring meetings — so nothing gets lost between conversations.
>
> - One note per person / team / meeting (e.g. `Agenda — Alex`, `Agenda — 1:1 with Manager`, `Agenda — Family`).
> - Jot items as they come up; review before the meeting and check off.
> - RSVP or include in your next interaction.
