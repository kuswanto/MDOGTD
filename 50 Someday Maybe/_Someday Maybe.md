# 🌟 Someday Maybe

> [!tip]- How to use
> Anything you're **not committing to right now** but don't want to lose — ideas, dreams, trips, home projects, side hustles.
>
> - Draft freely; no commitment, no deadline pressure.
> - Before committing, transform a someday item into a [[30 Projects/_Projects|project]] with real next actions.
> - Review and prune this list in the **weekly review** — carrying it is optional.
