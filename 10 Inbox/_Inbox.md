# 📥 Inbox

> [!tip]- How to use
> Capture everything here. Get it out of your head — thoughts, requests, links, tasks, ideas. Nothing gets processed yet; it just collects.
>
> - Dump anything pressing into this space without judgement.
> - Use the **Inbox Item** template to keep capture structured (`Cmd/Ctrl + P` → "Templates: Insert Inbox Item").
> - Process it regularly: clarify each item into a **Next Action**, **Project**, **Waiting**, **Someday Maybe**, **Agenda**, **Area** or **Reference** note, then move it out of Inbox (update `status` or move the file).
